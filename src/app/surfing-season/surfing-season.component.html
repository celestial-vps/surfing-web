FAQ

Mostly we can surf all year in Bali. There’s two season you can surf in Bali which is on Summer and Raining season. Summer start from April until September and Raining season start from October until March. For the summer ( april till september) most we surf on west coast with off shore wind condition. For the raining season ( october till march) most we surf on East Coast and South Coast with off shore wind condition. Anytime you come to Bali for surf is always good time for surf and learn how to surf.

Is very safe to surf in Bali. We had all any surf level to surf. From beach break for beginners to intermediate level and reef break for intermediate to advance level. Is more important to bring surf coach/guide when you are first time surf in Bali to guide you better surf spot for your surf level. And that will make you feel comfortable and safe to surf in Bali

Kuta Reef, Sanur, Kuta Beach and more! Bali offers world-class waves for all abilities, with spots like Berawa Beach, Nusa Dua, and Canggu providing varied challenges. Be sure to check the conditions and tide for the best experience.
