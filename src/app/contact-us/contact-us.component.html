About Learn Surfing Bali

Born in Bali, Frengki has been surfing for over 15 years and spent 5 years as a senior instructor at a large surf school in Bali before starting Holy Surf Bali. He has bronze certificates in Life Saving Surf Rescue, advanced resuscitation, and senior first aid international standards. He is qualified to Australian surfing instructor Level 1 and Level 2. Frengki speaks English, Japanese, Indonesian, and Balinese. He is well-known among guests for his constant reminder to 'relax'! He loves helping people learn to surf and sharing the beautiful waters of his island.
