About Holy Surf Bali

Born in Bali, Frengki has been surfing for over {{year}} years and spent 5 years as a senior instructor at a large surf school in Bali before starting Holy Surf Bali. He holds bronze certificates in Life Saving Surf Rescue, advanced resuscitation, and senior first aid (international standards). Additionally, he is certified to Australian Surfing Instructor Level 1 and Level 2. Frengki speaks English, Japanese, Indonesian, and Balinese fluently and is known for his constant reminder to "relax"! He loves helping people learn to surf while sharing the beautiful waters of his island.
